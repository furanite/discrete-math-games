Discrete mathematics · three games

Learn to reason,
one proof at a time.

Logic, proofs, sets, functions, induction, number theory, counting, probability, graphs, recurrences, Boolean algebra and finite automata. Questions change every time you play. Synapse and The Sleeping Proof also have a full glossary, a timed exam mode, a mistakes review and a printable certificate.

Quiz and puzzles

Synapse

Eleven layers, each with a short briefing, five questions with full explanations and a hands-on challenge. Plus a 90-second Gauntlet.

Play Synapse → Story game

The Sleeping Proof

Travel through a sleeping mathematician’s mind and reason with the fallacies inside it until you can spare them. After the ending, an epilogue adds two more guardians.

Play The Sleeping Proof → Board puzzle

Neo-Turing

Bring a harbour city back online by wiring finite automata. Forty-two gates across six districts cover DFAs, NFAs, regular expressions, grammars, pushdown automata and Turing machines, and end with a proof that some problems no machine can solve.

Play Neo-Turing →
